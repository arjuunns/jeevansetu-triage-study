# Extension experiments: report

Branch `experiments-v3`, written on 19 Nov 2026. Every number below comes from
`research/results_ext/summary_ext.json` (generated by `research/analyze_ext.py`; the same numbers as
tables in `summary_ext.md`) unless another file is named. Percentages are as printed by the analysis
(one decimal, on cases with a valid answer). Nothing here is rounded or filled in by hand.

## 1. Summary

- **All runs finished:** 80 new runs, 0 incomplete (`summary_ext.json -> runs`, `incompleteRuns`).
  - Every run answered all of its cases except one: MedGemma 27B with the ESI guide on the handbook
    answered 148 of 149 (one fallback, case P011; see section 7).
- **Pre-registered handbook tests (H1 to H4):**
  - H1 (the reassuring notice raises under-triage): **supported**, all three models.
  - H2 (retrieval lowers under-triage): **partly supported**. It held for Llama 3.1 8B and
    Gemma 3 12B, not for Qwen2.5 7B.
  - H3 (the guide raises Gemma 3 12B's exact agreement): **supported**.
  - H4 (model size helps, medical training does not): **supported**.
- **The notice effect, exploratory on MIMIC in the paper, is now confirmed on an independent set.**
  On MIMIC, the two new wordings (neutral, caution) lowered under-triage for both 27B models
  compared with the reassuring sentence, but neither was better than showing no sentence at all.
- **The 8-bit Gemma 3 27B did not do better than the 4-bit build.** It under-triaged more often
  (37.2% to 42.5% on its own; 43.5% to 46.9% with the safety rules; both Holm p < 0.05). Exact
  agreement did not differ significantly. This contradicts a sentence in the paper's limitations
  (see section 8).
- **Twenty similar past patients as examples gave the highest LLM agreement on MIMIC so far.**
  - MedGemma 27B reached 54.6% with the safety rules and no notice, and 54.1% on its own with the
    examples. The paper's best setup was 52.2%.
  - The logistic regression (62.3%) is still ahead. For the 27B models, the gap in exact agreement
    is no longer significant after Holm adjustment, but their under-triage is still significantly
    higher than the regression's.
- **Guide and retrieval together (task 2) gave no model a clear gain** over the better of the two
  components alone.
- **Gemma 3 27B against MedGemma 27B:**
  - MIMIC: no difference survives Holm adjustment.
  - Handbook: MedGemma under-triaged more often in all 11 setups. The difference was
    Holm-significant in two of them (+ safety; + RAG).
  - Exact agreement never differed significantly on either set.

## 3. Pre-registered tests: ESI handbook, 149 cases

The analysis follows `research/preregistration_handbook.md` sections 5 to 7:
- Test: one-sided case-level sign-flip permutation, 20,000 permutations, seed 42.
- Multiple testing: Holm within each family.
- H4c is judged on its unadjusted p value.
- "Inconclusive" would apply below 142 valid answers. No test was inconclusive: every test had
  149 valid cases.

Source: `summary_ext.json -> handbook.preregistered.tests[]` (diff and CI are B minus A, in
percentage points).

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| H1a | qwen2.5:7b safety, no notice vs safety | under-triage | 7.4 | 14.1 | 6.7 [2.7, 11.4] | <0.001 | 0.002 | supported |
| H1b | gemma3:27b safety, no notice vs safety | under-triage | 5.4 | 9.4 | 4.0 [1.3, 7.4] | 0.015 | 0.015 | supported |
| H1c | medgemma:27b safety, no notice vs safety | under-triage | 11.4 | 17.4 | 6.0 [2.7, 10.1] | 0.002 | 0.003 | supported |

**Verdict per family** (`handbook.preregistered.families`):
- H1: supported.
- H2: partly supported. For H2a, under-triage went in the opposite direction (10.1% to 11.4%);
  the exploratory two-sided p value is in `tests[3].pTwoSidedExploratory`.
- H3: supported.
- H4: supported.

As the pre-registration says, H4c being supported means only that MedGemma 27B was not shown to be
better. It does not show that the two models are equal.

**Known risks, stated in advance (pre-registration section 9):**
- The handbook may be in the models' training data.
- The vignettes state reasoning cues that a triage record would not hold.
- The safety rules fire on 37 of 149 cases, with false alarms from negated words and from normal
  infant vital signs.
- The vignette appears twice in each prompt, because the pipeline splits the complaint at commas.

These risks apply to every handbook number.

## 4. Exploratory results: handbook

All in `summary_ext.md`, "Runs (handbook)" and "Handbook exploratory comparisons"
(`summary_ext.json -> runs[]`, `handbook.exploratory`).

- **Highest exact agreement:**
  - Gemma 3 27B with guide + RAG: 65.1% [57.7, 73.2].
  - MedGemma 27B with the guide: 64.2% (148 cases).
  - Gemma 3 27B with RAG: 63.1%.

## 8. What this means for the paper (for task 11, not done yet)

- **Limitations sentence:** the sentence "our results may understate what these models can do at
  full precision" (`paper/sections/06-discussion.tex`, Limitations) is not supported for
  Gemma 3 27B. At 8 bits, it under-triaged more often.
- **Safety notice:** the effect of the reassuring sentence is no longer only exploratory. It is
  confirmed on the handbook (H1). The recommendation to drop that sentence is now backed by a
  pre-registered test.
- **Similar past patients:**
  - Examples narrow the gap to the logistic regression for the 27B models. MedGemma 27B reached
    54.6%, the new best LLM number.
  - They did not close the gap in under-triage.
  - They also made Qwen2.5's under-triage worse.
- **Handbook against MIMIC:**
  - On their own, the 27B models reached quadratic kappa 0.73 (Gemma 3 27B) and 0.76 (MedGemma
    27B) on the handbook. On MIMIC, the paper's highest kappa in the six main setups was 0.38.
  - Exact agreement was only a few points higher on the handbook (53.0% and 53.7%, against 49.8%
    and 50.2%).
  - So on the handbook the errors are smaller, rather than less frequent. This fits the view that
    part of the low kappa on MIMIC comes from the single-nurse reference and the sparse triage
    record.
  - Possible memorisation of the handbook limits this comparison.
- **Proposed title:** "Local Language Models for Emergency Department Triage: Discovery on
  MIMIC-IV-ED and Pre-Registered Confirmation on ESI Handbook Cases".
